I LOVE GAMES!

Games have always been more than entertainment to me. They are where design, code, art storytelling, and music collide to create experiences that people can truly feel and remember.

My journey started in polytechnic, and since then I have been constantly exploring, learning, and building. From freelance work to personal projects, every opportunity became a way to sharpen my craft, gain real industry experience , and grow through both successes and failures.

I love the process because there is always another idea to experiment with, another system to refine, and another world waiting to be created. I am also always excited to collaborate with more like-minded people who share the same passion for creating meaningful interactive experiences.

— Gan Kah Loke
